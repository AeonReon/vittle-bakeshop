Our Story

A little bakeshop
doing big things

We're a small, fiercely independent bakeshop tucked into the heart of Portstewart's Promenade. Everything we bake is entirely naturally leavened — no shortcuts, no compromise, just proper craft baking with a whole lot of heart.

From flaky, laminated pastries to our famous cruffins, ice cream sliders and naturally fermented breads — every bake is made fresh each morning. If you're visiting the North Coast, we're the stop people tell their friends about.

We're dog-friendly, community-obsessed, and genuinely buzzing to serve you something that makes your morning.
